Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of what information AB.Phano Book Marketing collects, why it is collected, and how it is handled.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AB.Phano Book Marketing, operated by AB.Phano, handles personal information collected through this website and through direct email communication. By using this website or contacting me, you acknowledge the practices described below.

Information I Collect

I may collect information that you choose to provide, including your name, email address, website or author page, genre, publishing stage, marketing budget, book details, goals, and any other information you include in a message or form submission.

This website may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, and pages viewed. This information is used to understand website performance and improve the experience.

How I Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and continue conversations you initiate.
  • To prepare recommendations, audits, proposals, and marketing deliverables.
  • To deliver requested resources, such as the Book Launch Blueprint.
  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent misuse of the website.

Email Communication

Communication is primarily handled through email. Messages and attachments may be retained so that project decisions, recommendations, deliverables, and requests remain documented and searchable. I do not sell your email address or add you to a marketing list without an appropriate request or consent.

Forms and Service Providers

Website forms may be processed by Formspree or another form-delivery provider configured for this website. That provider may process the information needed to deliver your submission. External providers have their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies needed for basic functionality. If analytics, advertising, embedded content, or other non-essential technologies are added, this policy and the Cookie Policy will be updated to describe them.

Sharing Information

I do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers only when necessary to operate the website, process a form, provide an agreed service, or comply with law. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the website.

Retention and Security

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, ongoing client work, record keeping, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or information about how your data is used. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, email info@abphano.com. I may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Children's Privacy

This website and its services are intended for adults. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law.

Updates to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, services, technology, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@abphano.com.
