The Story Behind the Strategy

Why I chose to devote my career to book marketing.

I have always believed that the world is shaped by stories. The right book, placed in the right hands at the right moment, can change how a person sees themselves or remind them that they are not alone.

Early in my career, I watched extraordinary authors with exceptional manuscripts disappear into the vastness of online retail. They had the craft and the passion—but not a clear strategy for metadata, blurbs, email lists, or advertising.

That realization changed the direction of my professional life. I immersed myself in publishing analytics, reader psychology, advertising platforms, and the changing landscape of book discovery.

Today, I work with fiction and nonfiction authors across genres—from debut novelists to established writers managing a deep backlist. Every campaign is intentional, documented, and designed around the needs of the book and the people most likely to love it.

Email-only by choice. Every recommendation and decision stays documented, searchable, and easy to revisit.

My Philosophy

What I Believe About Book Marketing.

Marketing is connection, not manipulation.

The bridge between your story and the reader who needs it. At its best, marketing feels less like selling and more like a thoughtful introduction.

Data and creativity work better together.

Analytics reveal what is happening. Emotional intelligence explains why it matters. Every decision begins with evidence and ends with the reader in mind.

Honest feedback is professional respect.

I will tell you what is working and what is costing you sales—clearly, kindly, and without comfortable falsehoods.

Written communication protects both sides.

Strategy, decisions, and recommendations remain documented, searchable, and easy to revisit whenever you need them.

Every serious author deserves a fair shot.

You do not need a six-figure budget or millions of followers. You need a focused strategy and the willingness to show up consistently.
