Working exclusively through email is an intentional choice that benefits both of us in several important ways. First, everything is permanently documented. Every strategy, every recommendation, every audit finding, and every progress report exists as a written record you can reference at any time, forward to a collaborator, or revisit years later. Second, written communication forces clarity. When I have to write out my recommendations, I think more carefully about them. When you write out your questions, you often clarify your own thinking in the process. Third, email respects your time. You can read and respond when it suits your schedule, without needing to coordinate calendars or interrupt your writing time. Fourth, information does not get lost or misremembered the way it can with verbal conversations. Your entire marketing history lives in your inbox, searchable and accessible whenever you need it.

When you email me, describe what you have tried, and I will respond with a written analysis of why it likely did not work and outline a different approach that addresses the root causes rather than the symptoms.
