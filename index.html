A Reality Check

Let's Be Completely Honest About Why Most Books Do Not Sell.

You did everything right. You wrote the book. You edited it until your eyes blurred. You invested in a professional cover. You hit "publish" with tears in your eyes and champagne in your glass, and you waited for the world to notice.

And then came the silence. A few sales from friends and family. A handful of kind reviews. Then the algorithm buried your book under thousands of newer releases, and your daily sales flatlined to zero.

I need you to hear this clearly: your book is not the problem. The problem is that the publishing industry has undergone a seismic transformation, and most authors have not been given the tools or honest guidance they need to navigate it.

Traditional publishers are marketing fewer titles. Self publishing platforms give you freedom but zero built in audience. And the readers you need to reach are scrolling past generic "buy my book" posts without a second glance.

Hope is not a marketing strategy. A plan is. A real plan is specific, measurable, and tailored to your genre, your audience, your budget, and your timeline. It accounts for the way readers actually discover books in the modern marketplace. And it is built on data, not assumptions. That is exactly where I come in.

The Process

How We Will Work Together, Step by Step.

I believe in transparency at every stage. You should never have to wonder what I am doing, why I am doing it, or whether it is working.

1
Free Consultation via Email

The Initial Conversation

We start with a detailed email exchange about your book, your goals, your current marketing efforts, and your budget. I will ask you thoughtful questions and listen carefully to your answers. You will send me as much or as little information as you like, and I will respond with honest, actionable observations and initial recommendations. This entire process is documented in writing so you can reference it at any time. Everything stays in your inbox, organized and accessible.

2
Week 1

The Deep Dive Audit

If we decide to move forward together, I begin with a comprehensive audit of your current marketing ecosystem. This includes your Amazon listing, your book's metadata, your website, your social media presence, your email list, your past advertising data, your reviews, your comp titles, and your competitive landscape. I leave no stone unturned. You receive a detailed written audit report that you own permanently.

3
Weeks 2 and 3

The Custom Strategy Build

Based on the audit findings, I build a fully customized marketing strategy designed specifically for your book, genre, and goals. This is not a template. This is a bespoke plan that accounts for the unique strengths and challenges of your specific situation. You receive the full strategy as a comprehensive written document that you can review, question, and reference at any time.

4
Ongoing

Execution and Optimization

Once the strategy is approved, I handle the heavy lifting: launching ad campaigns, coordinating ARC teams, rewriting blurbs, building email funnels, managing launch week promotions. I monitor performance data daily, make real time adjustments, and send you regular written updates so you are never in the dark. Every decision, every result, every recommendation is documented in writing.

5
Monthly or Post Launch

Reporting and Long Term Planning

At the end of every month or campaign, I deliver a comprehensive written performance report that breaks down exactly what we achieved, what we spent, and what we learned. More importantly, I provide clear recommendations for next steps. Every report is a permanent document you can use to track your progress over time and inform future decisions.
