Cookie Policy

How this website may use cookies, browser storage, pixels, embedded content, and similar technologies.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AB.Phano Book Marketing may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit this website. It should be read together with the Privacy Policy.

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, tags, and scripts. They may help a site remember preferences, understand usage, support security, or provide embedded features.

How This Website Uses Technologies

The site may use technologies in the following categories:

  • Essential functionality: technologies needed for navigation, security, forms, accessibility, or basic site operation.
  • Preferences: technologies that remember a setting or improve how the site behaves on a later visit.
  • Analytics: technologies that help understand page visits, device types, traffic sources, and general site performance.
  • Embedded and third-party content: services such as fonts, icons, form processing, social links, or other external resources may process technical information when loaded or used.

Current Site Configuration

This website is designed to operate without advertising cookies. The contact and resource forms may send information to a form-processing provider. External fonts and icons are loaded from third-party content-delivery services. Social media links do not set cookies merely because they appear on the page, but the linked social platform may use its own technologies once you visit it.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block, delete, or restrict cookies through privacy settings. Blocking essential technologies may affect navigation or form behavior. You can also clear stored website data from your browser or use private browsing controls.

Third-Party Services

Third-party providers may have independent cookies and privacy practices. Their processing is governed by their own policies, not this website's policy. Review the relevant provider's information before submitting personal data or enabling optional features.

Do Not Track

Browsers may offer a Do Not Track setting. Because there is no single industry standard for interpreting that signal, this website may not respond to every browser implementation in the same way.

Updates

This policy may change when the website adds analytics, advertising, embedded media, consent controls, or other technologies. The effective date above will be updated when a material change is made.
